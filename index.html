About

Hi, I’m Anja, working under the artist name Kanja.

I see myself as a multidisciplinary artist with a background in design, exploring abstraction across different mediums. Painting has always been one of the most natural ways for me to express myself, particularly through watercolor, color, intuitive forms, and abstract compositions.

More recently, my practice has expanded into jewelry, where I translate the same abstract language into objects that can be worn. For me, it’s another way of exploring form, color, expression, and the relationship between art and the person carrying it.

Whether through painting, jewelry, or other forms, my work is driven by intuition, experimentation, and the joy of making.
